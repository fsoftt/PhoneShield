# Is your number shown wrongly?

You can ask us to **hide the names** linked to your number or to **review** whether it is marked as spam by mistake.
Hiding names does **not** change whether a number is marked as spam, so nobody can use it to clean a spam number.

## From the app (automatic)

In the app, go to **Account → Is your number shown wrongly?**. You prove the number is yours with an SMS code, and:

- hiding the names is applied right away;
- the spam review is done by a person, within the deadlines of Colombian law (inquiries within 10 business days,
  complaints within 15 business days). The reviewer never sees your number: only your reason and how many reports it
  has. If approved, earlier spam reports stop counting; new ones still count.

To prevent abuse, such as switching SIM cards again and again, each number, each account and each phone gets **one SMS
and one appeal per month**, and the account and the phone **three per year**. The account must be at least a week old,
and the app checks with Google Play that it is the original app on an unmodified phone. Your number is not linked to
your account.

## Without the app (manual)

If you do not use Tranqui or prefer not to install it, write to <span class="placeholder">[CONTACT EMAIL]</span> with:

- the number (with country code, for example +57 300 123 4567),
- what you want: hide the names or review the spam marking,
- optionally, why you think it is a mistake.

A person reviews every request and may ask you for proof that the number is yours. We answer within the same legal
deadlines.

## What we do with your data

- The number is not stored: as everywhere in Tranqui, we only keep its hash with a secret key.
- In the app, the code is sent by Firebase (Google), which processes the number to deliver the SMS. As soon as you
  confirm the code, we delete the session Firebase creates to verify it.
- Limits are counted separately for the number, the account and the phone, each with its own encrypted code, so there
  is no record of which account appealed for which number.
- If you leave an email, we use it only to answer and delete it once the request is resolved.
