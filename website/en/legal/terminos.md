## 1. The service

Tranqui identifies calls and helps block spam using information contributed by the community. It is free and open source (AGPL-3.0).

## 2. Requirements

You must be 18 or older, or have the authorization of your legal guardians.

## 3. Acceptable use

You may not:

- report numbers as spam knowing they are not, or coordinate false reports;
- use Tranqui to harass or identify people;
- try to extract data massively or automatically;
- use offensive labels or labels containing other people's personal data.

We may limit or close accounts that break these rules.

## 4. Community information

Names and spam markings come from what other users contribute. We do our best for them to be correct, but they can be
wrong. If your number is shown wrongly, [ask for a review](/en/guia/apelacion).

## 5. Availability

The service is provided "as is". If there is no connection or the service does not respond, calls ring normally.

Data processing is governed by the [Data processing policy](/en/legal/politica-de-tratamiento).
